FAQ

IT questions

Do you come on-site?

Yes, in the Los Angeles area. Most problems are solved faster remotely; on-site visits are billed at $75/hour.

Google Workspace or Microsoft 365?

Both work well. If your team lives in Word and Excel, Microsoft 365 is usually the better fit; otherwise Google Workspace is simpler. We'll recommend one on our call. You pay the provider directly.

Can you buy the laptops for us?

We'll recommend exactly what to buy and set it up when it arrives. You purchase devices directly so the warranty is in your name.

What's not included in monthly support?

Big projects like office moves, new networks or replacing many devices are quoted separately. You'll always get the price before we start.
