About Tectonic

Small business technology, built with care

Tectonic is a Los Angeles studio that builds websites, portals and AI tools, and keeps small offices running. You work directly with the person building your project.

The founder

Wai Yan Min

I'm a Burmese-American full-stack developer and IT support specialist in Los Angeles. I started Tectonic to give small businesses the same quality of technology big companies have, at a price that makes sense.

As volunteer CTO of MYTTA Foundation, a nonprofit immigration legal services organization, I rebuilt their website in four languages and built the staff portal they use every day. I also redesign and maintain online stores for small businesses, and I work on a college IT help desk, solving the same account, device and Wi-Fi problems every small office runs into.

Before tech, I worked in restaurants, manufacturing and construction. That's why I build things that are simple to use, explain everything in plain language, and answer my phone.
