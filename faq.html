Getting started

How quickly can you start?

Usually within one to two weeks of a signed quote. We take on a small number of projects at a time so each gets full attention.

What do you need from me?

A short description of your business, what you want to change, your timeline and a rough budget. Logos, photos and text help, but we can work from a napkin sketch.

What if I don't know what I need?

That's what the free call is for. We'll ask about your goals and recommend the smallest setup that does the job. No pressure and no commitment.

Do you work with clients outside Los Angeles?

Yes. Most projects are handled remotely by video call, email and text. On-site IT visits are available in the Los Angeles area.

Pricing & payment

Are the prices on the site final?

They're starting prices for a typical small business. After a short call you get a fixed written quote, and that's the price you pay unless you ask for more.

How does payment work?

Websites and smaller projects: 50% to start and 50% at launch. Portals and larger builds: 40% to start, 30% at the demo and 30% at launch. We accept bank transfer and PayPal.

What other costs should I expect?

Things like your domain, hosting, Shopify, Google Workspace and AI usage are billed to your own accounts at the provider's price. We'll list them in your quote so there are no surprises.

How many changes are included?

Two rounds of changes are included in every project. Further changes, or new features, are $75/hour or quoted separately.

Ownership

Who owns the website or portal?

You do. Once the final payment is made, the code, domain and accounts are yours. We set them up in your name from the start.

Can I move to another developer later?

Yes. Your code lives in your own GitHub account with simple notes, so another developer can pick it up.

Will you show my project in your portfolio?

Only with your permission. Private portals are never shown publicly.

AI

Which AI do you use?

We build with the Claude API from Anthropic, choosing a faster low-cost model or a more capable one depending on the job.

How much does AI usage cost?

It's billed to your own Anthropic account. A typical small-business assistant costs a few dollars to a few tens of dollars a month, and we set spending limits so there are no surprises.

Is my customers' information safe?

Data goes only to your own accounts, and Anthropic does not train its models on API data by default. Sensitive requests are handed to a person instead of answered by AI.

Support

What happens after launch?

Fixes are free for 30–60 days depending on your project. After that, a care plan covers updates, backups and edits, or we bill $75/hour when you need something.

Can I cancel a care plan or IT support?

Yes, with 30 days' notice. Unused hours don't roll over to the next month.

How do I reach you if something breaks?

Call or text +1 (323) 577-5706 or email contact@buildtectonic.com. Care-plan clients get priority.
