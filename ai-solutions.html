FAQ

AI questions

Will the AI make things up?

We limit it to your own information (services, prices, policies) and tell it to hand off when it doesn't know. We also test it on your real questions before launch and review conversations afterward.

Why Claude?

Claude is strong at following instructions, reading long documents and writing natural replies, and Anthropic's API lets us choose between faster low-cost models and more capable ones for each job.

Can it speak Burmese or Spanish?

Yes. The assistant can reply in the customer's language. For Burmese, a native speaker reviews the setup and sample answers.

Do I need a website to use these?

No. Document reading, automations and staff assistants work without a website. An AI receptionist can also run on messaging channels.
