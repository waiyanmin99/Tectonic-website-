FAQ

Website questions

What do you need from me to start?

Your logo (if you have one), photos, and a rough idea of what each page should say. If you don't have text, we'll draft it with you.

Can I edit the website myself?

Yes on the Growth and Store plans. You'll get a simple editor and a short training session. On Starter, small edits are covered by a care plan or billed hourly.

Who owns the website?

You do. The domain, hosting account and code are in your name once the final payment is made.

Do you work with Wix or Shopify?

Yes. If you already use Wix or Shopify and want to stay, we can redesign and maintain it there. You pay the platform directly.

What happens after launch?

Fixes are free for 30–60 days depending on your plan. After that, a care plan covers updates, backups and edits, or we bill $75/hour as needed.
