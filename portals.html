FAQ

Portal questions

Is client data safe?

Yes. Every user signs in, sees only their own records, and all traffic is encrypted. Files sit in secure cloud storage on your account, and care plans include daily backups and security updates.

Can the portal connect to the tools we already use?

Usually, yes: Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Stripe, Twilio SMS, Calendly and most tools with an API.

Can you add AI to the portal?

Yes. Popular add-ons are document reading for uploaded files and a staff assistant trained on your procedures.

What if we need changes after launch?

Portal care ($400/month) includes bug fixes and up to 4 hours of changes. Bigger new features are quoted separately so you always know the cost first.
