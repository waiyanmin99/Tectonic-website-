Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

This policy explains what information Tectonic ("we", "us") collects through this website and how we use it. Questions? Email contact@buildtectonic.com.

Information you give us

When you use the contact form, we collect what you enter: your name, email, and optionally your business name, phone number, the service you're interested in, your budget, your message and any estimate you built with the quote builder.

How we use it

  • To reply to you and prepare a quote
  • To deliver and support services you hire us for
  • To keep basic business records, such as invoices

We do not sell your information, and we do not add you to a mailing list without asking.

Services that help run this site

  • Formspree receives contact form submissions and forwards them to us by email.
  • Vercel hosts this website and may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and pages requested, for security and reliability.
  • Google Fonts provides the fonts on this site, so your browser requests them from Google.

This site does not use advertising trackers. The quote builder runs entirely in your browser.

Client project data

When we build or support systems for clients, client and customer data is stored in accounts the client owns. We access it only as needed to do the work, and we follow the client's instructions on how it is handled.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry emails for as long as needed to respond and follow up, and project records for as long as required for business and tax purposes.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the information you've sent us by emailing us. California residents may have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
